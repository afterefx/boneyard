Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Short version: Boneyard does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Boneyard is a mobile application for tracking scores in 4-player domino games, available on iOS and Android. It is developed and maintained as an independent project.

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through the app store listing for your platform.

2. Data we collect

We collect nothing. Boneyard does not collect, transmit, store on any server, or share any personal information. The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising SDK, and no backend server.

The only data the app handles is the game data you create yourself:

  • Player profiles (names, avatar choices, color choices)
  • Game records (round scores, game status, completion dates)
  • App preferences (theme selection)

All of this data is stored exclusively on your device using the platform's local database (SwiftData on iOS, Room on Android). It never leaves your device.

3. Device permissions

Boneyard does not request any device permissions. The app does not access your:

  • Camera or photo library
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Microphone
  • Network / internet connection
  • Notifications

4. Internet access

Boneyard is fully offline. The app does not make any network requests. It will function identically whether or not your device has internet connectivity.

5. Third-party services

Boneyard does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or cloud services. There are no SDKs from any third party included in the app.

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Those platforms' own privacy policies apply to the download and purchase process, but are independent of the app itself.

6. Children's privacy

Because Boneyard collects no personal information whatsoever, it presents no privacy risk to users of any age, including children under 13.

7. Data deletion

All game data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time in two ways:

  • In-app reset: Open Settings (gear icon) and tap Reset All Application Data to permanently erase all players, games, and scores.
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app from your device removes all data associated with it.

Because no data is ever sent to us, we have no data to delete on our end.

8. Data backup

Your device's built-in backup system (iCloud Backup on iOS, Google Backup on Android) may include the app's local database as part of a full device backup. This is controlled entirely by your device settings and is not something Boneyard initiates or manages.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change. We will not introduce data collection without updating this document and, where required by platform guidelines, notifying users in the app.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us through the app store listing for your platform.
