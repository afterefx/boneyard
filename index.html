Everything you need at the table

🎯

Automatic Round Tracking

Boneyard follows the 14-round Off-the-Spinner sequence automatically — Double-6 down to Double-0 and back. No setup required.

👑

Shaker Rotation

Tracks whose turn it is to shake and deal each round. No more arguments about who dealt last.

📊

Live Standings

Running totals and a collapsible standings board update instantly after every round so everyone knows where they stand.

⏸️

Pause & Resume

Need a break? Pause mid-game and pick up exactly where you left off. Multiple games can be in progress at once.

🕰️

Full Game History

Every completed game is saved with a round-by-round score breakdown. Review past matches anytime.

🔒

100% Private

All data lives on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no ads. Your game history belongs to you.
